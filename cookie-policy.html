Cookie Policy

About cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. Some technologies are necessary to deliver a service; others support analytics or advertising.

What this website uses

The current website code does not set first-party cookies or use local storage, advertising trackers or analytics scripts. There is no optional tracking to enable through a cookie banner. Netlify provides hosting, security and form processing and may process technical request information to deliver these services.

External fonts and images

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and images from Pexels. Your browser sends technical information, including your IP address and browser request details, to those providers when requesting their resources. These resource requests are distinct from cookies; this website does not deliberately enable advertising or analytics tracking through them.

Your controls

You can inspect, block or delete cookies in your browser settings and use browser privacy controls to restrict third-party requests. Blocking resources may affect fonts, images or form functionality. If optional tracking is introduced, this policy and the relevant choices must be updated before it is enabled where consent is required.
